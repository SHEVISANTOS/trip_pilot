FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is TripPilot AI free to use?

Yes. Planning a trip, comparing budgets and generating an itinerary is free. You book flights and hotels directly with the airline, hotel or supplier, the same as if you'd found them yourself.

How accurate are the prices?

TripPilot AI pulls real, live prices from flight and hotel providers whenever they have coverage for your route. When a real provider has nothing for a destination, it falls back to a regional or AI-generated estimate instead of leaving a gap. Always verify the final price and availability with the provider before booking.

Which destinations does it cover?

Any destination. Visa guidance comes from an open, MIT-licensed passport-index dataset covering passport-to-destination pairs worldwide.

Do I need an account?

No — building a plan works without signing up. Create an account (with email or Google) if you want to save trips, track a booking checklist, and export a PDF itinerary.

Can I plan a multi-city trip?

Yes. Add every city on your route, in order, and TripPilot AI builds one combined budget and itinerary across all of them.

Is my data sold or used for advertising?

No. TripPilot AI never sells your data or uses it for advertising — see the Privacy Policy for exactly which services it works with and what each one receives.
