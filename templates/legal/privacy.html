Who we are

TripPilot AI is operated by VOT MWANZA LIMITED, a company registered in Tanzania. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at {{ support_email }}.

What we collect

Account data. If you sign up directly, we collect your email address and password (stored encrypted, never in plain text). If you sign in with "Continue with Google," Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us — only after you approve this on Google's own sign-in screen.

Trip data. The destinations, dates, budget, nationality, travel style, interests and other details you enter into the planner, plus the itineraries, budget breakdowns and booking checklists we generate from them and that you choose to save.

Technical data. Basic server logs — IP address, browser type, pages requested — kept for security and debugging, not for tracking you across other sites.

Cookies. Only essential cookies: one to keep you signed in (session) and one to protect forms from cross-site request forgery (CSRF). We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. The following services help run TripPilot AI and each sees only what it needs to do its specific job:

  • Google — Sign-In, if you choose "Continue with Google," and Google Maps, for estimating transfer times between the airport and your hotel.
  • Google Gemini — asked to estimate things like local attractions only when no real travel data provider has coverage for a destination. It receives the destination name only, never your account details.
  • Brevo — sends transactional emails on our behalf, such as password-reset links. It receives the recipient's email address and the email content, nothing else.
  • Travel data providers — SerpApi, SearchApi.io, Travelpayouts, LiteAPI, StayAPI, OpenTripMap, eSIM Go, OpenStreetMap (Nominatim/OSRM) and an exchange-rate provider, used to fetch real flight, hotel, attraction, routing and currency data. Each receives only the search terms your trip needs — destination, dates, nationality, party size — never your account email or identity.

Google user data

Data we receive from Google is used only to sign you in and show your account — never sold, and never used for advertising. Our use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and trip data for as long as your account is active. To delete your account and associated data, email {{ support_email }} — we'll process the deletion within a reasonable time.

Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted password storage, HTTPS in transit — to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

TripPilot AI is not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect data from, children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the most recent version.

Contact
